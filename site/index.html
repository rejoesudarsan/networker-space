Lower level · 08:00–18:00

Make things, round the big tree.

An open work area, lit by a court where a big tree rises through two floors, with a seating platform round its trunk. The maker’s table is for building things by hand, and the daytime workshops run round it. Off the court, a private lounge for founders, open into the evening.

Ground floor · Walk-in café · 08:00–18:00

The café. Walk in from 08:00.

Anyone can walk in: breakfast, coffee and a table to work at, pay as you go. It opens onto the garden.

First floor · 18:00–23:00

Where the evenings happen.

From 18:00 the event room holds meetups, learning events and film nights: a low stage, a screen and tiered teak seats. Informal seating runs round the courtyard; private rooms off it take a meeting or a small group.

Second floor

Two quiet rooms, and the roof.

Two quiet rooms for founders who need a closed door to work, 08:00 to 18:00. The terrace takes a laptop by day; after 18:00, the grill and the rooftop parties.

After six, no work

Evenings, 18:00–23:00.

At six the desks close and the house turns to people: meetups of people building companies; learning evenings, a talk or a workshop, then questions; film nights on the event room’s screen; PlayStation, no agenda; and rooftop parties on the terrace, with the grill.

Founders, early teams and the people around startups. Members come in any evening; lifetime members bring their team and guests. The calendar is being set.
