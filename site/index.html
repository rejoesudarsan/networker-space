Lower level · 08:00–18:00

Make things, round the big tree.

An open work area, lit by a court where a big tree rises through two floors, with a seating platform round its trunk. The maker’s table is for building things by hand, and the daytime workshops run round it. Off the court, a private lounge for founders, open into the evening.

Ground floor · Walk-in café · 08:00–18:00

The café. Walk in from 08:00.

Anyone can walk in: breakfast, coffee and a table to work at, pay as you go. It opens onto the garden.

First floor · 18:00–23:00

Where the evenings happen.

From 18:00 the event room holds meetups, learning events and film nights: a low stage, a screen and tiered teak seats. Informal seating runs round the courtyard; private rooms off it take a meeting or a small group.

Second floor

Two quiet rooms, and the roof.

Two quiet rooms for founders who need a closed door to work, 08:00 to 18:00. The terrace takes a laptop by day; after 18:00, the grill and the rooftop parties.

After six, no work

Evenings, 18:00–23:00.

At six the desks close and the house turns to people: meetups of people building companies; learning evenings, a talk or a workshop, then questions; film nights on the event room’s screen; PlayStation, no agenda; and rooftop parties on the terrace, with the grill.

Founders, early teams and the people around startups. Members come in any evening; lifetime members bring their team and guests. The calendar is being set.

How joining works

Four steps in.

  1. Book a call: tell us what you’re building, and which way in fits.
  2. Visit the house: come by in the day and see the floors.
  3. Get your invite: we write to you with your membership and how to start.
  4. First day in: a desk, the maker’s table, and your first evening.

Walking in? Skip all four: the café opens at 08:00.

Questions

Short answers.

Can I just walk in?

Yes. The café on the ground floor is open to anyone from 08:00 to 18:00, and you pay as you go.

Can I work after six?

No. At 18:00 the desks close and the evening programme begins.

Is there a desk for me?

By day you work in the open work area on the lower level, or at a café table. Whether desks can be reserved is being decided.

Where do I take calls, or get quiet?

Private rooms off the first-floor courtyard take calls and meetings. Two quiet rooms on the second floor are for founders who need a closed door.

Can I bring my team or guests?

Lifetime members can. For members, the rules are being set.

Can I come to the evenings without a membership?

The evening programme comes with membership. Whether some evenings open to walk-ins is being decided.

What is the lifetime membership?

Everything a member has, plus the founders’ lounge and your team and guests. There are 20 places, and it starts with a call.

How much does it cost?

Walk-ins pay as they go. Membership prices are being set; we’ll go through them on a call.
