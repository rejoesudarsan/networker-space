# Fourthspace founding sponsorship PDF — page plan draft

## Purpose and constraints

External, forwardable sales PDF for a brand decision-maker. Ten pages, 1280×720. Plain sales copy. Lead with the signature drink/menu; use visuals only when they demonstrate a purchased benefit. Do not include internal launch gates, expansion logic or operating commentary. Do not invent partners, performance, contact details, prices beyond the approved annual fee, venue capacity or event attendance.

Offer: ₹15,00,000 per defined category for 12 months at the proposed HSR house; one signature coffee/mocktail with brand story and QR; one bounded merchandise bay with voluntary distribution of sponsor-supplied stock; four sponsor-led event-space uses per calendar quarter schedule, with 20% off pre-agreed event catering excluding taxes/service charges; category exclusivity; one two-week feature; weekly activity reporting and four quarterly reviews. First house opening 30 November 2026. Five founding categories.

Brand examples OpenAI, Razorpay, Notion and Postman are illustrative only. Every asset containing them must say: “Illustrative brand concepts. No current partnership or endorsement.” Use names, not logos or copied trade dress. All drink recipes, event examples and mockups are illustrative. No Corgi results or claims transfer to Fourthspace.

## Page 5 — Merchandise distribution

**Sales job:** Explain the second benefit without implying retail sales or data collection.

**Headline:** Your merchandise, stored and offered at Fourthspace.

**Copy:** You supply and replenish approved stock. Fourthspace stores it in one agreed bay and offers it voluntarily to walk-in guests matching transparent audience criteria agreed with you. Guests do not need to buy anything or exchange contact details. Distribution is not a retail-sales commitment.

**Visual:** Realistic shelf with small text-name examples for OpenAI, Razorpay and Notion, plus one highlighted empty bay labelled “YOUR BRAND HERE / APPROVED STOCK.” Include simple stock-flow labels: You supply → We store → Guest chooses.

**Guardrail:** On-asset illustrative/no-endorsement label. No real third-party product packaging or logos.

## Page 6 — Quarterly events

**Sales job:** Make the four annual event uses concrete without suggesting guaranteed attendance.

**Headline:** Four sponsor-led event-space uses per year.

**Copy:** One use per calendar quarter. You choose the audience and format; your team curates and runs the event. Fourthspace provides the agreed space and operating support. 20% off pre-agreed event catering; taxes and service charges are not discounted. Event production and additional staffing are separately priced. Dates, guest limit, setup and cancellation terms are confirmed in each event schedule. Unused quarterly uses expire.

**Visual:** Illustrative annual calendar: Q1 product demonstration; Q2 customer roundtable; Q3 community evening; Q4 product launch. Beside it, a poster: “YOUR BRAND / PRODUCT DISCUSSION / FOURTHSPACE HSR / DATE TO BE CONFIRMED.”

**Guardrail:** “Illustrative formats only. No events booked or attendance implied.” No capacity claims.

## Page 7 — Complete annual package

**Sales job:** Consolidate exactly what the buyer pays and receives.

**Headline:** The annual partnership.

**Commercial block:** ₹15,00,000 / one defined category / 12 months / Fourthspace HSR. Term starts when the agreed core benefits become available.

**Six inclusions:**
1. Signature drink: one coffee/mocktail, brand story and menu QR for 12 months.
2. Merchandise: one agreed bay and voluntary distribution of sponsor-supplied stock.
3. Events: four sponsor-led uses; 20% off pre-agreed catering excluding taxes/service charges.
4. Category exclusivity: one written category at the HSR house for the contracted term.
5. Two-week feature: one annual in-house feature amplifying the same drink and QR campaign; exact placements listed in the benefit schedule.
6. Reporting: weekly activity reports and four quarterly partnership reviews.

**Exclusions:** Additional drinks, extra events, production, dedicated staffing and bespoke activations are separately scoped and priced. Merchandise stock and replenishment are sponsor-supplied.

**Open commercial gap:** Payment schedule and price tax treatment are not supplied; do not invent them. Owner must provide before external circulation or the agreement must clearly govern them.

## Page 8 — Sponsor reassurance

**Sales job:** Explain exclusivity, approval and consent as buyer benefits, not internal rules.

**Headline:** Your category. Approved placements. Voluntary participation.

**Three blocks:**
- CATEGORY EXCLUSIVITY — Your category and its boundaries are written into the agreement for the HSR house and contracted term.
- APPROVED PRESENTATION — Both sides approve the final drink presentation, brand story and QR destination before launch. Placements follow the agreed annual benefit schedule.
- VISITOR CHOICE — Guests choose whether to scan, accept merchandise or attend. Individual contact details require explicit opt-in. Member, attendee and newsletter lists are not included.

**Visual:** Sample category card: “CATEGORY: [YOUR DEFINED CATEGORY] / PARTNER: YOUR BRAND / HOUSE: HSR / TERM: 12 MONTHS,” plus three placement thumbnails: menu, shelf and event.

**Guardrail:** No “operating framework,” “safeguards” or other internal terminology.

## Page 9 — Reporting

**Sales job:** Show the buyer the actual reporting artifact and separate activity from outcomes.

**Headline:** A weekly report your team can use.

**Visual:** Client-facing “Fourthspace Partner Activity Report” template labelled “TEMPLATE — NOT OPERATING DATA.” Fields use em dashes rather than invented numbers:
- Reporting week and partner/category.
- Menu-live status.
- Paid signature-drink orders where separately identified by POS.
- QR campaign visits.
- Merchandise units received/distributed/remaining.
- Verified event check-ins.
- Validated redemptions and explicit opt-in enquiries.
- Missing data and corrections.
Separate sponsor-supplied section: product use, trials or sales attribution supplied by the partner.

**Copy:** Weekly activity report on one fixed working day for the preceding period. Four quarterly reviews cover delivery, sponsor-supplied outcomes and next actions. Drink orders, QR visits and event attendance are different measures; none is presented automatically as a lead, conversion or revenue.

**Guardrail:** Aggregate reporting by default; individual details require explicit consent.
