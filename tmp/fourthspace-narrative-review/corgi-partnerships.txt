BEFORE YOUR FIRST POUR
A few
good answers.
Still curious?
Let’s talk.
How do we measure the return?
+
Start with your campaign goal. The overview reports actual partner-drink selections as a measure of product engagement. For a lead-generation goal, discuss a dedicated QR link and how your team will attribute visits and conversions. Drink selections alone are not a measure of leads, revenue, or financial ROI.
Can we create more than one drink?
+
We recommend choosing one signature drink. A single, recognizable recipe makes it easier for customers to remember your brand and ask for it again.
How do we develop our signature drink?
+
Bring us your brand and the experience you want to create. The Corgi team leads the drink development and branded presentation, working with you to bring your partnership to life.
What does the partnership include?
+
A signature drink and branded sleeve, menu placement, in-café brand touchpoints, and support from the Corgi team. Launch and content opportunities can be discussed as part of your campaign.
Explore the partnership.
How much does a partnership cost?
+
Email
b@corgi.com
for current pricing and availability. Let us know your preferred launch date and café locations so we can discuss the right setup.
Where can our drink launch?
+
Corgi has locations in San Francisco, New York City, London, and Atlanta, with more cafés on the way. Tell us the cities you’re interested in, and we’ll confirm the right locations and launch availability for your partnership.
