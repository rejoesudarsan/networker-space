FOURTHSPACE                                                                                      THE MEMBERSHIP




The room is only
the beginning.
Membership is a repeated practice of asking, offering,
meeting and returning—not a desk package.

ASK                          Say what would move your company forward: a pilot, a hire, a technical answer or a customer
                             conversation.


OFFER                        Make your experience useful to another founder. The house gets stronger when members create
                             value for each other.


RETURN                       Follow up, join the next circle, host what you know, and let relationships develop beyond one
                             introduction.

FOURTHSPACE                                                    TRUST AND ELIGIBILITY




Founders are members.
Not inventory.
The value of the house depends on being able to
participate without being harvested, pitched or
displayed.

Founder status confirmed through application and review.

The member directory is never supplied to sponsors.

Introductions require consent from both people.

No unsolicited pitching, scraping or bulk member messages.

Members can ignore sponsor activity without losing benefits.
