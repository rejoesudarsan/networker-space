# Fourthspace Founder Membership — Operating v1

**Status:** Operating v1; first house opens 30 November 2026  
**Product:** One founder-only membership for Fourthspace HSR  
**Price:** ₹2,00,000 including year one; ₹1,00,000 annual renewal  
**Capacity:** 100 active founder members, subject to venue and hosting review

## 1. What the member is buying

Membership buys repeated, trusted access to other founders and a house system that helps members make useful professional encounters. It does not buy guaranteed funding, customers, hires, introductions, or free food and beverage.

The public café remains open to non-founders. Membership is the only paid individual tier; there are no Silver, Gold, Platinum, or lifetime tiers.

## 2. Eligibility and sales process

1. **Application:** applicant describes what they are currently building, what they need, and what they can offer other founders.
2. **Founder review:** Membership Lead confirms founder status through the application, available evidence, references where useful, and a conversation. Do not require company registration when a genuine early-stage founder has not registered yet.
3. **Fit conversation:** explain the professional-opportunity purpose, founder-only rule, paid F&B, guest limits, community code, and lack of guaranteed outcomes.
4. **Decision:** target five working days after the conversation.
5. **Payment and agreement:** collect the first-year fee and signed membership/privacy terms before activating access.
6. **Onboarding:** complete profile, access setup, house orientation, first ask/offer, and first event selection within seven days of activation.

Non-founders can use the public café and attend selected public events. Sponsors do not receive membership automatically.

## 3. Annual entitlement

| Benefit | Operating limit |
|---|---|
| Founder-house access | 08:00–23:00, subject to room and program schedules |
| Quiet founder rooms | 08:00–18:00; book in advance |
| Meeting rooms | Four included room-hours per member per month; maximum two hours per booking; no rollover |
| Founder profile and private member directory | Opt-in; visible to members, never supplied to sponsors |
| Ask/offer board | Post hiring, pilots, customer discovery, technical help, cofounder search, fundraising preparation, and other founder needs |
| Facilitated introductions | One request per member per month; both sides must consent; no outcome guarantee or rollover |
| House-led founder programming | Four sessions per month: circles, breakfasts, demos, or clinics |
| Member-hosted programming | Up to four approved member-hosted slots per month |
| Member hosting | Apply for a recurring or one-off format; curation and capacity apply |
| Guest access | Two guest day passes per month for meetings/work; event access separately stated; no rollover |
| Café F&B | Paid separately unless a specific event says otherwise |
| Renewal | ₹1,00,000 annually; invitation 30 days before expiry; member must remain in good standing |

Any access suspension, refund, pause, transfer, cancellation, or termination rule must appear in the signed agreement after legal review. Do not improvise those rules at the door.

## 4. House allocation

- **Ground:** public café, selected public events, discovery, and paid F&B.
- **Lower level:** founder work, maker activity, workshops, and member lounge.
- **First floor:** bookable meetings, founder circles, shared meals, and the main evening event program.
- **Second floor:** quiet founder rooms and small hosted gatherings. Terrace use depends on safety, noise, occupancy, and neighbourhood review.
- **Between visits:** asks, offers, introductions, and hosting continue through simple community tools. Do not build a custom app for launch.

Room capacity and use are proposed until the physical review is complete.

## 5. Weekly operating rhythm

**Daily**
- Check access, room bookings, guest passes, and event registrations.
- Record paid café transactions separately from member attendance.
- Review new asks/offers and route any introduction request to the Community Host.
- Keep sponsor activity out of founder-only areas unless explicitly scheduled and relevant.

**Weekly**
- Hold one house-led founder session on average, supporting the four-session monthly entitlement.
- Review upcoming member-hosted events, consent requests, room demand, and member issues.
- Make only introductions that both people have accepted.
- Publish the next two weeks of programming.

**Monthly**
- Reset room-hours, guest passes, and introduction-request allowances; unused allowances expire.
- Deliver four house-led sessions and make up to four member-hosted slots available.
- Review active members, attendance, repeat participation, introduction acceptance, event capacity, and complaints.
- Report voluntary member outcomes only when the member supplies them; never convert an introduction into a claimed opportunity automatically.

**Quarterly**
- Review hosting quality, member mix, room pressure, program usefulness, and commercial intrusions.
- Admit the next cohort only if current service levels and physical capacity support it.
- Check that no sponsor has received the member directory or unrestricted community access.

## 6. Roles

- **Membership Lead:** eligibility, sales records, agreements, payments, onboarding, renewals, and member-good-standing decisions.
- **Community Host:** profiles, asks/offers, two-sided introductions, programming, member hosts, and outcome follow-up.
- **House Operations:** access, room inventory, guest entry, capacity, safety, maintenance, and incident handling.
- **F&B Lead:** café billing, event catering charges, menu availability, and F&B reconciliation.
- **Sponsorship Lead:** sponsor activity kept separate from membership records; only explicit opt-ins passed to sponsors.

One person may hold several roles at launch, but every task needs one named owner before sale.

## 7. Launch cohort and expansion

Start with up to **25 paid founding members**, not all 100 at once. Review the first 30 days of onboarding, participation, introductions, room demand, and community quality. Add further cohorts of up to 25 only after the team can serve existing members properly. The cap remains 100 active members, not 100 new sales every year.

At 100 new first-year memberships, gross collected membership revenue is **₹2 crore**. At 100 subsequent annual renewals, gross collected renewal revenue is **₹1 crore**. These are full-capacity calculations before costs, taxes, refunds, non-renewals, or timing differences—not forecasts or profit.

## 8. Member trust rules

- No unsolicited pitching, scraping, bulk messages, or exporting the directory.
- No selling or sharing member details with sponsors without separate, explicit consent.
- No guarantee of funding, jobs, customers, cofounders, or sponsor outcomes.
- No sponsor control of founder programming or member admission.
- Members may ignore sponsor activity without losing membership benefits.
- Document warnings, incident review, suspension, and appeal in the membership agreement.

## 9. Launch gate

Do not take payment until the team has: verified venue access and safety; named role owners; opened application and payment records; finalized membership and privacy terms; loaded the first month of programming; tested booking and guest workflows; established paid F&B billing; and prepared a delay/refund process.

Track a single source of truth for member status: applied, reviewed, offered, paid, active, suspended, expired, renewed, or cancelled. Never describe applicants or directory entries as paid members.
