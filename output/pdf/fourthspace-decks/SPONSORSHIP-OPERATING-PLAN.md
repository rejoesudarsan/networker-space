# Fourthspace Brand Sponsorship — Operating v1

**Status:** Operating v1; first house opens 30 November 2026  
**Product:** Category-exclusive annual partnership at Fourthspace HSR  
**Price:** ₹15,00,000 per category per year  
**Launch inventory:** Five founding categories  
**Future ceiling:** 20 categories only after operating and member-experience gates pass

## 1. What the sponsor is buying

The core product is one co-created signature coffee or mocktail that represents the sponsor on the Fourthspace menu. Its name and story express the brand; its QR link takes interested guests to the sponsor’s website or offer. Merchandise distribution, quarterly events, a two-week feature, and reporting extend that central partnership.

The sponsorship does not buy founder membership, the member directory, guaranteed leads, product adoption, sales, or control of community programming. Its value is a repeatable physical experience, useful brand participation, and reporting that separates activity from outcomes.

## 2. Annual core package

| Benefit | Operating entitlement |
|---|---|
| **01 / Signature serve** | One co-created coffee or mocktail on the menu for 12 months, with an approved name, brand story, and sponsor-specific QR destination |
| **02 / Merchandise distribution** | One bounded storage/shelf bay; sponsor supplies approved stock for voluntary gifting to walk-in guests matching agreed transparent audience criteria |
| **03 / Event-space use** | One sponsor-led use per calendar quarter; four per year; unused quarters expire |
| Event F&B | 20% off pre-agreed event catering; taxes and service charges are not discounted |
| Category exclusivity | One written category at the HSR house for 12 months; included products, exclusions, parent brands, and conflicts recorded in a category registry |
| Featured rotation | One two-week annual feature that amplifies the signature partnership |
| Reporting | One weekly activity report plus four quarterly partnership reviews, using metrics actually collected |

The first house opens on **30 November 2026**. The 12-month term starts when the agreed core benefits become available. Pre-opening sales must be conditional contracts with payment milestones, opening-delay treatment, refunds or make-goods, and termination rules.

Additional menu items, extra events, event production, dedicated staff, demos, recurring office hours, bespoke research, and enhanced reporting are separately scoped and priced.

## 3. Attention and calendar

**Signature menu presence:** each current partner has one approved signature serve. Menu capacity, recipe complexity, ingredient availability, preparation time, and service quality must pass review before adding partners.

**Merchandise distribution:** each partner has one bounded bay. The sponsor supplies stock; Fourthspace follows the agreed gifting and replenishment workflow. This is a distribution entitlement, not a retail-sales commitment.

**Rotating support:** one featured sponsor at a time. Five launch partners use ten feature-weeks in total, leaving the rest for house and community content. At 20 partners, two-week features use 40 weeks, leaving 12 house weeks.

**Events:** sponsors curate, fund, staff, and run their events. House production is an optional paid service. At launch, five partners receive 20 annual event uses. At full future inventory, 20 partners receive 80. The operating calendar reserves two sponsor evenings per week across 48 active sponsor-event weeks: 96 theoretical annual slots and a 16-slot buffer before house programming. Actual capacity still requires the venue calendar and safety review.

Member programming remains sponsor-free unless a specific useful partnership is approved. Not every house interaction carries a brand.

## 4. Sales and onboarding

1. **Category-fit conversation:** identify the sponsor’s objective, desired audience, useful offer, category boundary, and internal decision-makers.
2. **Written proposal:** state the exact category, annual fee, signature-serve concept, benefit schedule, dates, responsibilities, measurement, and exclusions.
3. **Fit and conflict review:** approve brand safety, product suitability, category overlap, visitor relevance, and operational load.
4. **Agreement and payment:** execute category, sponsorship, privacy, cancellation, and delay schedules. Do not call verbal interest a signed partner.
5. **Onboarding:** collect brand assets, story, QR destination, serve brief, merchandise plan, audience criteria, event preferences, billing details, and reporting contacts.
6. **Launch:** approve the recipe and menu story; test QR links and consent; brief staff; reconcile the first report before expanding activity.

Maintain sales status separately: lead, qualified, proposal sent, verbal interest, contract sent, signed, invoiced, paid, live, expired, or renewed.

## 5. Activation workflow

### Signature serve and QR
- F&B co-creates one coffee or mocktail with the sponsor and approves its recipe, ingredients, allergens, price, costs, availability, and service process.
- Sponsor supplies the brand story and approved claims; Fourthspace controls final menu layout.
- The menu QR links to the agreed sponsor website or offer. Test the destination, tracking, and visitor disclosure before launch.
- Record menu-live dates and, where the POS supports it, paid item orders. Do not equate orders with unique guests or QR visits with conversions.

### Merchandise distribution
- House Operations controls the bay’s dimensions, placement, and maximum simultaneous brands.
- Sponsor owns stock cost, shipping, replenishment, damaged/expired goods, and end-of-term removal unless the agreement says otherwise.
- Agree transparent, non-sensitive audience criteria with the sponsor. Staff offer gifts voluntarily; they do not covertly profile guests or require contact details.
- Record units received, distributed, damaged, and remaining. Individual recipient details require separate consent.

### Two-week supporting feature
- Sponsor chooses one objective: product discovery, research, voluntary enquiries, community association, or another agreed purpose.
- Use the feature to amplify the signature serve and its QR journey rather than adding unrelated activations.
- Staff-assisted demos, trials, office hours, and introductions require separate scheduling and consent.
- Record feature dates and delivered assets in the sponsor ledger.

### Quarterly sponsor-led event
- **T–28 days:** request date; confirm category conflicts, room, duration, staffing, and current legal guest cap.
- **T–21:** approve audience, format, guest plan, catering direction, public/private status, and any paid production.
- **T–14:** approve content, promotion, accessibility, security, and event responsibilities.
- **T–7:** lock final headcount, pre-agreed catering, deposits, setup, AV, and cancellation status.
- **Event day:** sponsor curates and runs the event; House Operations controls venue, safety, setup, and incident escalation.
- **T+5 working days:** reconcile actual attendance, catering charges, tracked engagement, opt-ins, incidents, and make-goods.

The event guest cap is not published until the venue safety and occupancy review sets it. The 20% discount applies only to agreed catering, not an unlimited event bill.

## 6. Measurement and consent

Report each step separately:

1. Paid signature-serve orders where the POS separately identifies the item, with counting rules.
2. Menu-QR campaign visits and feature participation.
3. Merchandise units distributed and verified event attendance.
4. Validated offer redemptions and explicit opt-in enquiries or introductions.
5. Sponsor-side trials, usage, or sales only when supplied by the sponsor.

A drink order is not a unique guest; a scan is not a lead; an introduction is not an opportunity; a redemption is not incremental revenue. Reports use aggregate data by default. Individual details require separate consent naming purpose and recipient. Do not supply attendee, member-directory, or newsletter lists.

**Weekly activity report:** send on one fixed working day each week for the preceding reporting period. Include menu-live status, signature-serve orders where verified, QR visits, merchandise distributed, feature activity, event attendance, validated redemptions, explicit opt-ins, missing data, and corrections. Show zero or unavailable activity honestly; do not fill a quiet week with unsupported estimates.

**Quarterly partnership review:** four per year. Review delivery against the annual contract, sponsor-side outcomes where supplied, serve and event economics, member feedback, and next-quarter actions. Weekly reporting does not replace this commercial review.

## 7. Roles

- **Sponsorship Lead:** category registry, proposals, contracts, payment status, sponsor onboarding, renewals, and sponsor ledger.
- **Activation Lead:** signature-serve coordination, feature calendar, brand story, QR links, merchandise plan, sponsor staff brief, and delivery evidence.
- **House Operations:** merchandise storage and distribution records, room capacity, event approval, setup, safety, staffing, cleaning, and incident response.
- **F&B Lead:** signature recipe, costing, ingredients, allergens, pricing, menu availability, catering packages, discount application, deposits, billing, and reconciliation.
- **Community Host:** member safeguards, voluntary participation, and two-sided consent for introductions.
- **Reporting Owner:** metric definitions, source records, deduplication, corrections, retention, weekly report delivery, and quarterly review preparation.

## 8. Expansion from five to 20

Do not sell the next category group until all current partners have completed two quarters or an equivalent delivery review and every gate passes:

- At least 90% of scheduled benefits delivered or formally made good.
- 100% of weekly activity reports delivered on time.
- Zero unresolved safety or privacy incidents.
- Zero unsolicited member-data-sharing incidents.
- Positive direct sponsor contribution after menu, activation, event-support, discount, sales, and reporting costs.
- Member-feedback review shows no unmanaged commercial intrusion.
- Menu, merchandise, event, and feature capacity reviews support additional partners.

At five annual sponsors, gross contracted annual value is **₹75 lakh**. At 20, it is **₹3 crore**. Both figures are before costs, taxes, discounts, non-renewals, and timing differences; neither is profit or guaranteed cash.

## 9. Launch gate

Do not take payment until the category registry is ready; venue safety and capacity are verified; build and opening runway are funded under downside scenarios; sponsorship/privacy/delay terms are legally reviewed; signature recipes, stories, prices, costs, and QR destinations are approved; merchandise and event workflows are tested; and every fulfillment role has a named owner.

Do not advertise all 20 categories as current inventory. Start with five, deliver the package, and expand only when the house can protect founders and serve sponsors at the same time.
