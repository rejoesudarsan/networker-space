why HSR

HSR Layout was never designed for this. The Bangalore Development Authority laid it out in 1985 as seven sectors of housing plots, main roads running north to south, cross roads running east to west. Part of it sits on land reclaimed from Agara lake. It was a housing scheme that turned out to be unusually good at absorbing a startup district nobody had planned for it.

what actually happened

Koramangala got there first and then got tired. By 2020 YourStory was describing it as traffic, broken roads and cramped buildings, and noting that the startup ecosystem had been moving to HSR for about five years. HSR is two kilometres south. It had the newer buildings and the parking.

Udaan is the origin story people tell. Founded in 2016 by three ex-Flipkart executives, based in HSR from day one, at a valuation between $2.5 billion and $3 billion within three years. Cure.fit started in a bungalow on the same cross road and grew into a 32,550 square foot office. KhataBook moved its office from Mumbai to HSR in early 2019. Its co-founder gave two reasons for picking HSR: the engineering talent already living around it, and cheaper office space. That cluster earned 17th Cross Road in Sector 3 the name Unicorn Street. No published source attributes the name to anybody, and at the time exactly one of the companies on it was a unicorn.

What made the cluster stick was not the anchors. In 2020 Oliveboard's founder said VCs had spent three years telling him their portfolio companies were in HSR, and Cure.fit's head of engineering said the reason was simply that people at a startup do not want to live far from the office. Third Wave Coffee opened its HSR outlet around mid 2018 and sat empty most of the day, filling up only after office hours. By January 2020 a reporter sitting there mid-week counted four empty tables. The rest held freelancers, founders and a few VCs.

the boring reasons

Rent was the first one, and it is the one that has aged worst. In 2020 Oliveboard was paying Rs 78 per square foot a month here. In June 2026 myHQ put HSR bare shell at Rs 85 to Rs 120 per square foot against Koramangala at Rs 90 to Rs 120. Identical at the top of the range, Rs 5 apart at the bottom. That is nothing like the 20 to 30 percent the listing sites keep repeating, a figure myHQ's own HSR page states four times without a source. Pronto told the Economic Times in March 2026 that it pays around Rs 8 lakh a month. HSR is not cheap. It is merely cheaper than the alternatives that come with a worse commute.

Residential rent moved the same way. A Sector 4 two bedroom went for Rs 19,500 a month in 2017 and Rs 25,000 by 2020. Listing portals now quote Rs 22,000 to Rs 35,000 for a semi furnished two bedroom, while 263 renters reporting their actual rent put the median at Rs 37,000. I read that gap as the difference between what is advertised and what people who moved recently are paying.

The rest is geography. The Outer Ring Road is right there, which means Bellandur, Marathahalli and Sarjapur are in your hiring radius. Electronic City is south down Hosur Road. The Blue Line metro station is still under construction and slated for around December 2026, so for now the answer is still a cab.

the AI chapter

In June 2024 Analytics India Magazine ran a piece headlined as a petition to rename HSR to HSR.AI. What prompted it was a single Urban Vault building holding Factors, Loop, Raga, Frinks and Actyv. Urban Vault now runs more than 40 properties. In January 2026 Karnataka approved CATS, a Rs 20 crore applied AI centre, at the KEONICS facility in HSR. Approved, not open. Colliers expects HSR and the micro market next to it to take up two to three million square feet of Grade A space.

how this was counted

One rule decides the list: the company has a physical office in HSR Layout, and something says so that I can point at. A company's own site, an MCA or GST filing, a news photograph, or a LinkedIn location block. A virtual office is not an office. A residential flat used as a registration address is not an office. A directory listing that exists only to sell leads is not evidence of anything. Agencies are out, and so are coworking operators, which are on the map as places instead.

That rule leaves 118 companies, two of which have since moved out and stay in the list marked as such. Every address was geocoded through OpenStreetMap's Nominatim: 11 of them resolved to the door, the rest to the road, and every entry says which. That is why pins in the same building fan out instead of stacking, and why the fan shrinks back toward the true address as you zoom in.

The whole thing is open at /hsr-startups.json, CC BY 4.0, companies and places together. If an entry is wrong, and some of them will be, tell me: akshathg7@gmail.com or an issue at github.com/akshatgoel07/new-portfolio/issues.

how many startups are in HSR Layout

118, by the rule above: a company with a physical office inside HSR Layout, evidenced by a source I can cite. Two of them have since left, so 116 are there today. That is the number this page can defend.

The number you will hear is 17,000 startups in HSR. I went looking for where it came from and found nothing. No report, no post, no press release says it. Here is what does exist. DPIIT had recognised 13,649 startups across all of Bengaluru district, rural and urban, as of October 2024. Karnataka as a whole reached 24,026 by June 2026. Seventeen thousand sits between those two, which is why I think it is a state number wearing a neighbourhood's name.
