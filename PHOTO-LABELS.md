- **There are two double-height spaces.**
  1. **Basement Court 1 (14'6" × 12')**: double height, with the big tree, shown as "Double height" on the ground plan. The wooden-balustrade stair (#20) connects it to the ground floor from inside. The workroom / gallery is beside it (Office/Den on the plan, "Studio/Gallery" in the CAD file).
  2. **First-floor "Double HT Court" (13'10" × 12')**: the courtyard with the olive tree, flagstones and nest pendants. It rises past the second floor (the void with the crossed lines) to the skylight.
- **The garage and the office have separate entrances.** The garage is reached down the ramp. The office is reached through the basement's "Open to sky court" and its outside stair.
- The ground and first floors are similar flats, each with its own kitchen, dining, family and living rooms. The first floor can be reached from inside or by its own outside stair and porch (#6, #4).
- Rooms still marked L can stay as they are; the walls are what matter for the model.
