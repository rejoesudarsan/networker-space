The house in one page

Four levels, two double-height spaces, two separate basement entrances, and a first floor reachable from inside or by its own outside stair.

BasementGarage down the ramp. Office suite with its own entrance through an open-to-sky court. Double-height Court 1 with the big tree and the workroom / gallery; an inside stair up to the ground floor.
GroundEntrance court and Foyer 1. Living 1 with the teak floor, facing the open entrance and the front lawn. Family, dining, galley kitchen, two bedrooms, puja, caretaker.
