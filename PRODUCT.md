# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Vite + Three.js (WebGL), static build. User asked for a WebGL experience in the spirit of bruno-simon.com / kodeclubs.com / igloo.inc and left the stack to us. Static output deploys anywhere (Vercel/Netlify).

## Users

Prospective members of a private club for startup people (founders, early teams, operators, people around startups) in Bengaluru. They arrive from a referral, social post or event, want to understand what the space is and which membership fits them, and then book an intro call.

## Product Purpose

Networker Space is a private club focused on startup activity, housed in a converted multi-level house (plans in `plans/`, filename indicates HSR Layout). The website lets prospects get a feel for the space, see the three memberships and what each one needs, and book a call with the team. Success = booked calls from well-matched prospects.

## Positioning

A house, not an office floor: four levels with distinct characters (lower-level work rooms and courts, ground-floor gardens and living spaces, first-floor library and dining, rooftop terrace). By day it runs like a walk-in café; by night it becomes a programmed club for startup people.

## Operating Context

- **Day, 8:00 to 18:00:** functions like a normal walk-in café.
- **Evening, 18:00 to 23:00:** programmed activities and events. User described events as "a three-day thing"; interpreted as three evening-programme days per week. **Unconfirmed.**
- Access to evening activities depends on membership tier.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three memberships. User's working labels: Silver, Gold, Platinum. Final names are open, and the user asked us to propose them.
  - **Silver:** mostly walk-in access; can book a table, mostly on a walk-in basis.
  - **Gold:** own quiet spaces, plus game rooms and other spaces. The user asked for research on what this audience needs.
  - **Platinum:** more access, and can bring their team and guests (friends, etc.).
- Each membership must state what it **needs** (requirements/eligibility) as well as what it includes.
- Booking: Cal.com / Calendly link (URL not yet supplied).
- Room uses per floor are not decided. The plan drawings carry the original house labels (bedroom, puja, etc.), which are not the club layout.

## Brand Commitments

Name: "Networker Space" (working name, from the project folder). No logo, palette or voice supplied.

## Evidence on Hand

- `plans/HSR-plans-Model.pdf`: four floor plans (basement, ground, first, second).
- `plans/plans-AS-BUILT.dwg`: CAD, not inspected.
- No photos, prices, address, testimonials, member names or partner logos. None of these may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Show the house itself; the real plans are the proof.
2. Make the tier differences legible in one glance: what you get and what it takes.
3. Every path ends at "Book a call".
4. Day café, night club: the 8:00–18:00 / 18:00–23:00 rhythm is part of the offer.
