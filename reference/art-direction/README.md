# Space homepage — art direction boards

Eight OpenAI image-generated PNG boards, each 1536 × 1024, made on 2026-10-04. These are visual directions, not measured reconstructions or homepage implementation. Read alongside [the photo labels](../../PHOTO-LABELS.md) and [the Igloo reference notes](../igloo-inc/README.md). The reference contributes one subject, quiet corner chrome, and scene changes concealed by fog. Board 01 anchors 02, 06 and 07 directly; floor scenes also use the actual room photographs. The exterior camera stays fixed, while the floor scenes intentionally move upward to expose their spaces. Fog opacity increases for the transition; matching atmosphere does not imply numerically identical volumetric density. [PROMPTS.json](PROMPTS.json) records generation and refinement prompts.

**01 — [Dawn exterior](01-dawn-exterior.png).** The complete closed house stands alone at 6 am in grey-pink haze, with two sparse gulmohars, teak reveals and a grey gate. Grounded in [p19](../../docs/house-understanding/img/p19.jpg) for the street façade and [p05](../../docs/house-understanding/img/p05.jpg) for the gate. Keep the stepped roofline, restrained blossoms, solitary framing and generous fog margins. Reject the first attempt's dense foreground canopy, extra planting and sun flare; these were removed. Treat the cleaned-up façade and symmetric tree placement as art direction, not as surveyed geometry. The real address is 1172; visible numerals were omitted to honour the no-text image requirement.

**02 — [Fog transition](02-fog-transition.png).** The exact dawn composition dissolves into warm pale fog, leaving only a faint upper stepped roof silhouette. Grounded indirectly in p19 and p05 through board 01. Keep the empty beat and continuity of roof position; reject readable rooms, foreground objects, ghost labels and dramatic cloud effects. This frame deliberately has higher opacity than the settled scenes so the next floor can appear unseen.

**03 — [Café morning](03-cafe-morning.png).** An open-top ground-floor living-room section holds two small café tables on polished teak, with brass lanterns and morning light through glazed teak doors. Grounded in [p25](../../docs/house-understanding/img/p25.jpg); the lawn relationship is supported by the supplied Living 1 context and photo labels for p27 and p21. Keep the floor reflections, sparse furniture, recognizable glazing and empty fog beyond. Reject additional café equipment, crowds and the initial closed ceiling; the final section removes it. The supporting beam, table settings and section boundary are invented presentation devices, and the lawn is mostly concealed by fog.

**04 — [Courtyard afternoon](04-courtyard-afternoon.png).** A downward view into the first-floor double-height court at 4 pm shows an olive tree in a concrete planter, sandstone flagstones, three woven nest pendants, teak louvred doors and one group of three. Grounded directly in [p11](../../docs/house-understanding/img/p11.jpg) and [p01](../../docs/house-understanding/img/p01.jpg). Keep the compact court, tree-to-door relationship, varied pendant heights and soft light through the skylight frame. Reject an enlarged plaza, a second tree or confusion with the separate basement court. Seating, people and exposed section edges are conceptual additions; validate final roof/skylight construction against the house.

**05 — [Terrace sundown](05-terrace-sundown.png).** The second-floor terrace at 6:30 pm has terracotta paving, a timber bench, white parapet and four people talking beneath one sparse strand of warm lights, against an orange-to-violet atmosphere. Grounded in [p33](../../docs/house-understanding/img/p33.jpg). Keep the long terrace proportion, bench, parapet openings, four-person scale and fog around its base. Reject neighbors, drying racks, party furnishings and the initial sun disc; the final framing pulls back and removes the sun. String lights and occupants are proposed use, not photo evidence. The side-room roof is still prominent and touches the left image edge; reduce that mass or widen the camera further in the eventual 3D sequence.

**06 — [Night club](06-night-club.png).** At 10 pm the same closed house and street camera return, every visible window glowing warm amber, with delicate roof string lights against dark blue fog and a faint street plane. Grounded in p19 and p05 through the dawn anchor. Keep matching window placement, contained warmth and low street contrast. Reject neon, club signs, people, cars or another building. Window glow and roof lights are an imagined nighttime treatment; their physical installation and real light spill remain to be checked.

**07 — [HUD and type](07-hud-and-type.png).** The dawn hero carries only four small monospace corner labels: SPACE, Book a call, Scroll and 06:00 IST. Grounded in the same p19/p05 exterior, with chrome restraint informed by the supplied Igloo frames and README. Keep the empty centre and clear gap between chrome and architecture. Reject central headlines, panels, survey lines and labels pinned to the house. SPACE is a provisional typeset wordmark, and the clock is illustrative static text. An image edit can introduce small pixel differences from board 01; use the original hero as the actual compositing background during implementation.

Grounded in p19/p05 for plaster and gate, p25 and p11/p01 for teak and sandstone, [p16](../../docs/house-understanding/img/p16.jpg) for terrazzo, [p15](../../docs/house-understanding/img/p15.jpg) for red oxide, [p08](../../docs/house-understanding/img/p08.jpg) for steel, p33 for terracotta and [p24](../../docs/house-understanding/img/p24.jpg) for entrance foliage/green bench context. Keep the muted texture range and quiet layout; reject treating these hex values as measured physical material colours. Material values are proposed approximations. Fog labels are actual mean RGB samples from the left-side atmosphere of final boards 01–06 (x 4–15%, y 40–60%): #D1BFC2, #CFBDC0, #BDB0B2, #CEC0C0, #786C7D and #1C2D4C. Generated chip colours are illustrative and may not reproduce those numeric values exactly.
