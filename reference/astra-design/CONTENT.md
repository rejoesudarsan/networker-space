## Utility and recovery

- Loading: `Preparing the house` / `Continue without motion →`; no made-up percentage.
- Load failure: `The house is taking longer to arrive.` / `Try again` / `Continue without motion →`.
- Static/reduced motion: `Explore at your own pace.` / `Previous` / `Next` / `Book a call`.
- Explore closing control: `Close ×`; Escape also closes.
- Sound control: `Sound off` / `Sound on`; default off.
- 404: `This room isn’t here.` / `The page may have moved.` / `Back to Space →`.
- Booking unavailable: `Book a call` / `Call booking is not available yet.` / `Please check back soon.` / `Back to Space →`.
- Booking handoff when configured: `Choose a time to meet.` / `Continue to booking →` / `Back to Space →`.

Do not show “Booked” unless a verified booking provider callback confirms it. No provider name, duration, availability, contact address or calendar slots are invented in these designs.
