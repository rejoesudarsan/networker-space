Scope: single-page Persuade site (site/index.html). Visitor: prospective member of a startup-focused private club. Job: understand the house, pick a membership, book a call.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The house is a Bengaluru street of painted signboards you walk past. Scrolling is the clock running 8:00 to 23:00, and each floor is a stop. At the end the four painted plates stack into the building. This refuses the dark-glass "immersive WebGL" landing page and the generic coworking pricing grid.

OWN-WORLD: Hand-painted enamel signage. Sign-yellow ground by day, deepening to ultramarine night after 18:00. Signal red, enamel green, ultramarine, black and enamel white are flat fills with hard black keylines and offset shadows, never gradients or glass. Bungee lettering for the boards, Anek Latin for body copy, and bilingual Kannada sub-lines like real shop boards. The floor plates are painted slabs carrying the real plan linework.

STORY: Café by day, club by night, in a real four-level house. Three memberships, each saying what you get and what it takes. Book a call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed yellow street. A big painted board reads NETWORKER SPACE with a Kannada line, and an hours strip reads "Café 8–6 · Club 6–11". The ground-floor plate floats in 3D at right. A red "Book a call" board button sits top right and under the headline.

FORM: Painted signboard street, candidate 3 of 7; seed 49404e34 (degraded roll). Signature interaction: a scroll clock drives a horizontal pass across the four floor plates and a day-to-night sky, then the plates assemble into the stacked building.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Unresolved: tier names (proposed Walk-In / Regular / House), pricing, Cal.com URL, floor uses (proposed), evening programme days.
